********** MONOTONIC STACK **********

--> When to Use:
 1.When we want comparison in particular direction like how far/how many days till etc


--> Types:
 Basic type:
  1. Next Greater Element(NGE)
  2. Next Smaller Element(NSE)
  3. Previous Greater Element(PGE)
  4. Previous Smaller Element(PSE)
 
--> Note: 
 1. All other problems like histogram, rain water, subarray mins/maxs are based on these types
