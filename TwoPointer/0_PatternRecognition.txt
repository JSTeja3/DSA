********** TWO POINTER **********

--> When to Use:
 1. Pairs in an array whose sum is K (Sorted)
 2. Palindrome
 3. Window of Subarray/Substring which matching a condition
 4. Floyd's cycle (slow and fast pointer)

--> Invariant:
 Invariant is a rule or set of conditions that is/are always true at any specific point 
 of an algorithm before and after loop

--> Window Problem:
 1. Variable Window: 
 ex: find the largest subarray whose sum is k

 2. Fixed Window:
 ex: find max sum of subarray whose size is k

 --> Rule/Property of Sliding Window:
   1. When a window becomes invalid, moving the left pointer forward should help make it valid again.
   not may be make it valid in next iterations
 -->Types of Sliding Window:
   1. atmost k (<=k): atmost k slips
   2. aleast k (>=k): atleast k unique
   3. equals k (==k): equals to k size
   4. delayed shrinking: longest repeating characters replacement
